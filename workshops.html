Where Does AI Fit in R Package Risk-Based Assessment?: A Working Session Toward a Community Whitepaper

Date & Time

Tuesday, November 10, 2026
3:40 PM - 4:40 PM Eastern (1 hour)

Format: Live workshop

Description

The R Validation Hub’s risk-based framework – evaluating R packages on purpose, maintenance practices, community usage, and testing – has become foundational for using R in regulated submissions. At the same time AI tooling has matured enough that some consider it is a plausible candidate for helping in some tasks such as assisting assessors in reviewing maintenance signals at scale, drafting or evaluating remediation tests, flagging changes across package versions.

AI also raises an issue in return: how should risk assessment treat packages or contributions that are themselves AI-generated or AI-assisted?

This workshop is designed to gather people with hands-on experience in R package validation, regulated environments, and applied AI tooling for a working discussion.

Where AI can realistically help within the existing framework

Where AI-assisted evaluation introduces new risks that would themselves need validation

What a minimal, practical pilot of AI- assisted risk assessment could look like

Session notes and points of consensus will be captured and used as the starting point for a community whitepaper

Topic: Evaluation, Observability, Responsible AI, and Governance

From Lab to Production: Shipping R-Powered Drug Discovery Models as Apps on an Enterprise AI/Data Platform

Date & Time

Wednesday, November 11, 2026
12:35 PM - 1:35 PM Eastern (1 hour)

Format: Live tutorial

Description

Most machine learning models built in R for drug discovery never leave the researcher’s laptop. This talk is a case study of taking R-based models from exploratory lab code to a governed, production AI application on an enterprise data platform.

At the center is the R pipeline: reproducible data preprocessing, molecular feature engineering, model training, and scoring, packaged to run reliably against enterprise data. I’ll show how this pipeline is version-controlled, containerized, and served behind a Streamlit frontend so scientists can query predictions without touching code.

I’ll also cover the realities of shipping AI in a regulated domain: secure deployment, private or managed models that keep proprietary chemistry in-house, production monitoring, and cost control at scale. Attendees will leave with a concrete blueprint for operationalizing R machine learning, plus the practices that make R pipelines reproducible, auditable, and trusted in a regulated environment.

Topic: Production AI and Industry Case Studies
